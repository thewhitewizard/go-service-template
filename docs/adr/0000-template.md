# ADR-NNNN — Short title of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Deprecated
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Slice**: (the `docs/SPEC.md` §6 entry this decision governs)

## Context

What problem? What constraints (performance, capacity, cost, deadline)? What forces a
decision *now* rather than later?

## Decision

The decision taken, in one or two affirmative sentences.

> You write this file, so there is no gate to perform here: fill the decision in and set
> `Status: Accepted`. Leave it `Proposed` only while you are genuinely undecided — a slice
> whose governing ADR is still `Proposed` is not implementable, and that is the only thing
> the status is for.

## Options considered

- **Option A** — description, upsides, downsides.
- **Option B** — description, upsides, downsides.

## Consequences

What the decision implies — **including the downsides accepted**. A good ADR names
explicitly what is lost, not only what is gained. An ADR listing only benefits was
written to justify a choice, not to record one, and it is worthless in eighteen months
when someone asks why.

## Mitigations

How the downsides listed above are limited.

## Verification

How we will know the decision was right: a metric, a benchmark, a test. A decision with
no verification cannot be revisited on evidence, only on opinion.
